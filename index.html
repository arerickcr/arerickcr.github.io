Research and publications

My coauthored work spans the massive Schwinger model, Yang–Lee quantum criticality, and the Sachdev–Ye–Kitaev model. I connect theoretical predictions with numerical calculations, with particular interest in precision, finite-size effects, and spectral structure.

  • Precision study of the massive Schwinger model near quantum criticalityDMRG, critical-mass estimation, and cross-validation between physical criteria.
  • Yang–Lee Quantum Criticality in Various DimensionsNon-Hermitian spectra and comparison with conformal predictions.
  • Precise low-temperature expansions for the Sachdev–Ye–Kitaev modelLow-temperature behavior in an interacting many-body model.

Background

I am from Ecuador and hold bachelor's degrees in mathematics and physics from Universidad San Francisco de Quito. Before joining CMU, I completed the ICTP Postgraduate Diploma Programme and received the 2021 Best Performing Diploma Student Award in the HECAP section.

My background also includes experimental condensed matter physics and teaching mathematics and physics. I enjoy explaining technical ideas and working across disciplines.
